# Eleven tasks for computer-use agents — article and evidence

Shashwat Mahalanobis.

Open `index.html` or `dark.html` for the dark article, or `paper.html` for the paper variation. The Dark / Paper switch keeps both versions easy to compare. They share the same revised task-first content and interactive task explorer. The article, styles, fonts and case images are local, so this copy works offline. Hosted trajectory links still need an internet connection. `evidence/article.pdf` is the printable edition when included.

- The interactive theater includes verified checkpoint frames for all selections and ten optional full hosted embeds. Full hosted playback requires internet access.
- `research.html`: task notes, findings and corrected claims.
- `validity.html`: grader, environment and reward-hacking boundaries by family.
- `claims.html`: 24 qualified claims and their evidence/limits.
- `source-index.html`: source documents and provenance.
- `evidence/<task>.zip`: each selected run’s verifier evidence and provenance README.
- `evidence/store-blindspot-reproduction.json`: three memory-only negative probes.

This collection presents eleven computer-use tasks and their observed results. It is not a controlled leaderboard or a certification that all retained packages are valid benchmarks. Missing historical contracts and public/local differences are explicit. The Games XL and store-term selected hosted URLs were unavailable; their exact local recordings were reviewed. No paid trials were launched for this investigation.

The original catalog, work dossier and session-authorship ledger are local background sources, not copied wholesale into the shareable packet. Relevant historical reports are retained as evidence; some contain interpretations corrected by this review. Read the research before reusing those claims.

The dark variation uses a black canvas, sans-serif typography, a borderless contents sidebar and a dimmed, tinted collage of task screenshots. The earlier paper variation remains available. Design references are credited in the source index.
